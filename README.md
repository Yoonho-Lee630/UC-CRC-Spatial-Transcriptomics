# Spatial Transcriptomic Analysis of Colitis-Associated Colorectal Cancer

## Overview

This repository contains the analysis code used to investigate the spatial organization of tumor cell states and their interactions with immune and stromal compartments in colitis-associated colorectal cancer (C-CRC).

We performed integrated spatial transcriptomic profiling using 10x Xenium in situ and 10x Visium spatial transcriptomics on the same tissue sections from seven tumor regions obtained from three patients with C-CRC.

The study characterizes proliferating and invasive tumor cell states, their spatial associations with cancer-associated fibroblasts (CAFs) and SPP1⁺ macrophages, and their broader clinical relevance in sporadic colorectal cancer (S-CRC).
